What is this app?

A complete billing & accounting app built for small and medium shops and businesses. No computer or extra hardware needed — just your Android phone.

Everything from making a bill to the balance sheet happens inside the same app. All data is stored on the phone itself, so billing never stops even without internet. Internet is only needed if you want to take a backup.

05

Online ordering & the customer app

Alongside the shop owner's app there is a dedicated ordering system for customers — just your own server, no extra monthly cost.

Catalog

Online catalog

The shop owner picks which items to show online, optionally with a special offer price and a shop banner image, and uploads it.

Order

Customer ordering

Customers browse the catalog and place an order through the customer app (or a web page opened by scanning a QR code) — with location, a note, and a photo attachment. Past orders can be reordered from Order History.

Live

Live notifications

The shop owner is notified the moment a new order comes in, and the customer is notified the moment its status changes (accepted/ready/delivered) — both inside the app.

Chat

Chat

Shop owner and customer can message each other directly about an order — on both sides, right inside the app, with its own notification.

Discover

Nearby shops & referral

Customers can browse nearby shops by category. Shop owners get a referral link to invite other shop owners.

No app

Install-free option

For customers who don't want to install an Android app, and for iPhone users — the same ordering flow is also available as a web page (PWA) opened straight in the browser.

06

Other features worth mentioning

Tax

GST & VAT

Full Indian GST — CGST/SGST, inter-state IGST, composition scheme, cess, HSN, bill numbers per financial year. Also Saudi ZATCA and UAE VAT formats for the Gulf.

Print

Printing

Print directly on a Bluetooth thermal printer (58mm / 80mm), or A4 on a WiFi printer. PDFs come out in the same width chosen in Settings.

Share

WhatsApp share

Send the bill as a PDF straight to the customer's WhatsApp number. Add a UPI QR code to the bill so the customer can scan and pay.

Backup

Backup & sync

Back up to the phone, push to a server. Merge data when two phones are used for the same shop. One-tap repair if duplicates show up.

Input

Fast entry

Barcode scanning, reading a list with the camera (OCR), voice typing (including Malayalam), and handwriting — all supported.

Calculator

Billing calculator

For quickly adding up during a rush. Every total can carry a label, be saved under a customer, and turned into a real bill later. Full-screen view to show the customer.

Users

Multiple users

Separate logins for staff. Control who can do what — e.g. can create a bill but can't delete one.

Stock

Stock control

Batch number and expiry date, two units (box/piece), low-stock alerts, stock as of any given day.

Frequently asked questions

Is internet needed?

Not for billing or accounting — everything is stored on the phone. Internet is only needed for pushing a backup to the server, sending a bill on WhatsApp, and for online ordering and chat.

Does online ordering need its own server?

Yes — a small, low-cost PHP hosting plan is enough. Just upload one PHP file to it; no separate database server is required.

Can old accounts be brought in?

Yes. Items and customers can be imported from an Excel / CSV file. A customer's old due amount can be added as an opening balance.

Can it be used on two phones at once?

Yes. Billing can happen on each phone separately, then merging the backups (Merge) combines the data from both. Every bill carries a number that shows which phone it came from.

Can a shop without GST use it?

Yes. Turning GST off in Settings hides tax on the bill. A separate "No Tax Invoice" format is also available if needed.

Is a printer required?

No. Without a printer, the bill can just be sent as a PDF on WhatsApp. Any Bluetooth thermal printer can be added later.

Can a wrong bill be corrected?

Yes. Open it from the bill list to edit or delete it. Stock and the customer's account update automatically. User permissions can control who is allowed to change what.

After the demo

Once the app is installed with the shop's details and a few items added, they can start billing that same day. It helps to call once in the first week as a reminder to take a backup.
